Position Title: Senior Software Developer
Experience: 5–7 Years
Location: Bengaluru, Karnataka, India / Hybrid
Department: Product Engineering

ROLE OVERVIEW

We are looking for a Senior Software Developer to join our product engineering
team and help build, enhance, and support business-critical web applications.
The successful candidate will be comfortable working across the software
development lifecycle, from understanding requirements and designing solutions
through development, testing, deployment, and production support.

This is a hands-on engineering position. The person will work closely with
other developers, QA engineers, product managers, and technical leads.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

• Design, develop, test, and maintain production-quality software applications.
• Translate functional requirements and product specifications into technical
  solutions.
• Develop REST APIs and integrate applications with internal and external
  services.
• Participate in code reviews and contribute to engineering standards.
• Investigate production issues, identify root causes, and implement sustainable
  fixes.
• Write automated unit and integration tests for new and existing functionality.
• Participate in sprint planning, backlog refinement, daily stand-ups, reviews,
  and retrospectives.
• Collaborate with Product, QA, UX, and DevOps teams to deliver features on
  schedule.
• Contribute to application performance, reliability, security, and
  maintainability improvements.
• Document technical decisions, APIs, deployment procedures, and operational
  knowledge.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

• 5+ years of professional software development experience.
• Strong experience developing production web applications.
• Strong proficiency in at least one of Java, Python, C#, or TypeScript/JavaScript.
• Experience developing RESTful APIs and integrating third-party or internal
  services.
• Experience with relational databases such as PostgreSQL, MySQL, or SQL Server.
• Hands-on experience with Git and modern software development workflows.
• Experience writing automated tests.
• Practical experience working in Agile/Scrum development teams.
• Strong debugging and problem-solving ability.
• Good written and verbal communication skills.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

• Experience with React, Angular, or another modern frontend framework.
• Experience with Docker and cloud platforms such as AWS or Azure.
• Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines.
• Experience with messaging systems such as Kafka or RabbitMQ.
• Exposure to application monitoring and observability tools.
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology,
  or a related field.

SUCCESS IN THIS ROLE

Success will be measured by the quality and reliability of delivered software,
ability to resolve complex engineering problems, contribution to team
engineering practices, and ability to deliver commitments consistently.
